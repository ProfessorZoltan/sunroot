# Decisions

Rules that [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) leaves open, and the choice made for each. Every number named here
lives in `src/content/willow-reach.json`; the choices below are about _how_ rules work. Each one is
covered by a test in `tests/`.

## Changes to the design

Rule changes made on purpose, with the evidence. DESIGN.md is not edited; these override it.

| #   | Design says                                                        | Now                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| D1  | "Food beyond storage rots into scraps."                            | Food beyond storage rots into **biomass** (`rules.rotsInto`). Scraps come only from people, as "Scraps come from people" intends. Biomass still feeds composters and digesters but never becomes clutter. Setting `rotsInto` back to `scraps` restores the original rule. | Rot made 36–71% of all scraps. 1,171 of 3,000 non-random bot runs collapsed from clutter; after the change, none did, and non-random completion rose from 7–94% to 100%. Bots still build about 4 composters per run to keep up with people's scraps. See [balance/history/01-before-clutter-fix.md](balance/history/01-before-clutter-fix.md) and [balance/history/02-before-combos.md](balance/history/02-before-combos.md). |
| D2  | Lantern of the Valley: "no shortfall in any slot for a full year". | A full calendar year, spring to winter, without a shortfall **and with 30 or more citizens** at the end of every season of it (`goal.minCitizens`).                                                                                                                       | Read literally, every competent bot met it in year 2 and half the random runs in year 1, when a camp of 6 has nothing to power. With 30 citizens it is met around year 8. See [balance/baseline-report.md](balance/baseline-report.md).                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| D3  | Thriving Commons: "40 citizens at wellbeing 70 or more".           | **50** citizens at wellbeing 70 or more.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | At 40, every non-random bot met it without trying. At 50, the bots that grow fastest still do (greedyFood 100%, greedyEnergy 81%), and the balanced bot, which aims elsewhere, rarely does (13%).                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

## Where the design disagrees with itself

The working rules say to raise these rather than guess. Each was resolved the way the Year 1
walkthrough (the golden test) requires, and should be confirmed or changed in the design.

| #   | Conflict                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Resolution                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| C1  | The season order runs flexible consumers (step 5) before storage charges (step 6), but the Cell Bank alternative says the bank "uses the workshop's spare day energy" and ends the year at 7 materials, which needs the bank to charge first. | Before flexible consumers run, storage **reserves** the spare day energy it needs to cover a night shortfall it can already see. Flexible consumers use what is left; storage then charges from what is still spare (step 6 as written).                                                                                                                                          | Covering demand is a need, not a spare. With the literal order the workshop would run once and the year would end at 10 materials, with a blackout. |
| C2  | "Energy, storage, water and nature buildings need no workers", but the building table gives the Tree Nursery 1 worker.                                                                                                                        | 1 worker, as in the table.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | The table is the more specific source.                                                                                                              |
| C3  | "Unprocessed scraps become clutter", but the walkthrough has 2 clutter appear in **summer**, not spring.                                                                                                                                      | Scraps wait one season. In step 9, scraps still unprocessed from last season become clutter, then this season's new scraps are added.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Spring's 2 scraps become summer's 2 clutter; in autumn the new composter eats them and "clutter stops growing".                                     |
| C4  | A workshop can run on clutter, and in autumn it has spare energy and 2 clutter, but the walkthrough ends autumn at 10 materials (one salvage run, no clutter run).                                                                            | Each workshop has a recipe setting (`salvage` by default, or `clutter`), changed with the `setRecipe` command. | C6  | Silt: "floodplain farms gain +50% food in summer and autumn", but the second farm, built on the floodplain in summer, makes plain 4 and 5.                                                                                                    | Silt belongs to the farms standing when the flood comes, not to the tiles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Otherwise autumn ends at 21 food.                                                                                                                   |
| C7  | Rounding order between the silt multiplier and the compost +1.                                                                                                                                                                                | Multipliers first (all multiplied together, rounded down once per building), then flat bonuses and penalties.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Autumn's silted farm makes ⌊5 × 1.5⌋ + 1 = 8, giving 19 food. | C10 | Winter Garden: "full winter food", but a powered greenhouse already makes its full 3 food in winter.                                                                                                                                          | **Decided by the designer:** no winter heat demand, and 4 food in winter instead of 3.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The original text did nothing.                                                                                                                      |

Food is the farm's yield −1 in each season it makes food (never below 0), silt after the −1, rounded down. Energy is the canopy's 3 / 4 / 2 / 1 by day (shade applies, as to any canopy). No low-river loss. It counts as solar for the Mixed Grid. | "+3 in summer" was shorthand. The benefit is a saved tile and drought immunity.                                                                     |

## Season structure

| Topic             | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forecast          | `forecast.event` is this season's event (announced during the previous season); `forecast.next` is next season's. The calendar is fixed per biome: flood, low river, storms, freeze.                                                                        |
| Draft timing      | A card can be picked at any point in the season, but must be picked before the season ends. Building before picking is allowed.                                                                                                                             |
| New buildings     | Buildings placed during a season work in that same season (the walkthrough's spring workshop runs at once). Orchards are the exception (they mature).                                                                                                       |
| Undo              | Undo is replay-based: the run keeps the state at the start of the season plus the commands since. Undo replays all but the last. Undo restores the random generator too, so undoing a reroll and rerolling again deals the same cards (no reroll-scumming). |
| Guided first year | Run option `guided`: year 1 uses the fixed offers in `guidedYear` (orchard in spring, River Wheel / Cell Bank / Heat Well in winter). The golden test plays the guided year. From year 2 offers are random.                                                 |
| Run end           | After 48 seasons (`complete`) or when wellbeing reaches 0 (`collapsed`). Commands are rejected once a run ends.                                                                                                                                             |
| Step 10           | The combo discovery check is a stub until Milestone 6.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Energy

| Topic              | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Heat               | Heat is not a separate supply. It is paid in the same slot as energy, 1 for 1, or from a Heat Well's stored heat. The Camp's and Cottages' winter heat is due at night; the Greenhouse's is due by day, with the rest of its demand. Free heat (Solar Thermal Collector) and Heat Pumps pay heat before direct energy; see [proposals/heat-routes.md](proposals/heat-routes.md).                                |
| Storage discharge  | Storage only discharges into shortfalls (step 7). Stored heat pays only heat demand.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Cell Bank          | Charges from day energy only. Stores up to 4 and returns 3 for every 4 (like the Pumped Reservoir), rounded down: 3 stored covers a 2 shortfall. Empties at the end of the season.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Heat Well          | Charges from spare energy in any slot and any season (1 energy = 1 heat), up to 6. Loses 1 heat at the end of every season. A kiln run next to it adds 1 heat for free.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Pumped Reservoir   | Charges from any spare energy, up to 12 stored, returns 3 for every 4, and keeps its charge between seasons. Era 3 applies to when the blueprint can be drafted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Reservation order  | For a night shortfall, heat wells are charged first (for the heat part, 1:1), then energy storage (4:3).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Flexible consumers | Each workshop or kiln run takes 2 energy from one slot, day first, then night. Buildings run in priority order.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Blackouts          | Settled per slot, day first. Buildings with demand in that slot are shut off lowest priority first; demand is worked out again after each one (heat pumps save less as heat demand falls) until it fits. A building shut off by day stays off at night and is unpowered for the season.                                                                                                                         |
| Priority           | One list per run, highest first. Default: homes first, in build order, then everything else in build order, so homes shut off last and older buildings get workers first. The player can reorder it (`setPriority`).                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Unpowered homes    | A home that is blacked out, or flood-damaged, counts as unpowered (−2 wellbeing).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Shading            | Tall things are the Wind Spire, Kiln, Seedbank Library and woodland tiles. A Solar Canopy next to any of them loses 1 per slot (only in slots where it produces), not 1 per tall neighbour. Canopies do not shade each other (see open question Q4).                                                                                                                                                            |
| Wind Spire         | −1 per slot for each other spire within 2 tiles. "Lowers nearby Harmony": −2 to the settlement's Harmony per spire, cancelled by a neighbouring Pollinator Meadow.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| River Wheel        | "+1 per slot next to a weir": +1 in each slot when adjacent to a Weir building. The low-river drop is already in the table (summer 1 / 1).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Biogas Digester    | Needs no workers (energy building). Runs automatically, up to 2 times, when 2 scraps or biomass are in stock (scraps first). Its energy goes to its assigned slot (night by default, `setDigesterSlot`).                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Mixed Grid         | **Decided by the designer (Q3, Q5).** Measured over the last 4 seasons of energy from built sources only: not the Founders' Camp, not storage releasing energy, not the bonus itself; biogas counts. Source types group by `sourceType` (an Agrivoltaic Field is solar). While it holds: no storm damage, and +1 energy in every slot (`rules.mixedGrid.bonusPerSlot`). The freeze damages nothing, on purpose. |

## Food, land and Harmony

| Topic              | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Harmony multiplier | Applies to food, biomass and knowledge yields. Not to energy, materials or salvage, so the energy slot puzzle stays readable.                                                                                                       |
| Farm on meadow     | −1 food in every season it produces (never below 0).                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Low river          | Affects Floodplain Farms only (orchards are deep-rooted), and halves food only (not biomass). Water means river, reservoir or a Fish Pond; distance is measured in tiles.                                                           |
| Neighbour bonuses  | Each kind of bonus reaches a building at most once (two apiaries next to one farm give +1, not +2). An apiary's 3 targets are taken in neighbour order: E, NE, NW, W, SW, SE.                                                       |
| Composter          | Takes up to 3 inputs, scraps first, then biomass, and makes ⌊inputs × 2 / 3⌋ compost. Each +1 food it gives a neighbour spends 1 compost from the store ("while compost lasts").                                                    |
| Orchard            | Produces from its third season (built in autumn → first food next spring). Its tile turns to meadow when it matures; it never downgrades woodland.                                                                                  |
| Weir               | Placed on a river tile. The 3 river tiles directly upstream become reservoir (still water for adjacency). A Fish Pond is downstream if it touches a river tile further down than the weir. Nothing can be built on reservoir tiles. |
| Tree Nursery       | Each season improves its least healthy neighbouring tile one step, ties broken by neighbour order. Only barren, scrub and meadow improve; hills, floodplain and ruins are not on the land-health ladder.                            |
| Compost spreading  | A player command (`spreadCompost`): 2 compost improves one tile one step.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Pollinator Meadow  | Built on barren, scrub, meadow or woodland. Raises its tile to meadow (never downgrades). Counts +1 Harmony on top of the meadow tile.                                                                                              |
| Salvage            | Salvage Yards stand only on ruins and draw from the ruin's 24 salvage. No other building can use a ruin tile.                                                                                                                       |
| Food rot           | After eating, food above storage (Camp 40 + 5 per Cottage) rots into biomass (D1).                                                                                                                                                  |

## People

| Topic          | Decision                                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Spare food     | Food produced this season minus food needed this season.                                                           |
| Growth check   | Uses wellbeing as it stood at the start of step 8, before this season's change. Growth never exceeds free housing. |
| Every need met | Every citizen fed and every home powered.                                                                          |
| Hunger         | Unfed citizens cost wellbeing but do not leave directly; people leave only below wellbeing 20.                     |
| Workers        | Citizens staff buildings in priority order. An unstaffed or damaged building does nothing and has no demand.       |
| Founders' Camp | Houses 6, stores 40 food, needs no workers.                                                                        |

## Events

| Topic         | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flooded area  | Every floodplain tile. With any weir, only the lowest half (rounded down) floods; the lowest ground is the tiles closest to the river. Several weirs do not halve it again.                                                  |
| Levees        | Protect floodplain tiles within 2 tiles of the levee from both damage and silt. Built on a land tile next to the river.                                                                                                      |
| Flood repairs | Damaged buildings are disabled for the flood season. At the start of the next season each is repaired automatically for 2 materials, in priority order; if materials run short it stays damaged and tries again next season. |
| Storms        | Unless the Mixed Grid bonus is active, one building (chosen with the run's seeded generator) on a hill that is not next to woodland is disabled for the season. No repair cost.                                              |

## Map generation

| Topic            | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shape            | A 12 × 10 hex rectangle (120 tiles). The river runs one tile per row from the north edge to the south edge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Banks            | Tiles next to the river are floodplain except on 3 short "bluffs": steep, dry banks drawn as **hills** right on the river. River wheels and kilns stand there safely, and a Pumped Reservoir can go there beside a weir's reservoir (with hills only at the valley edges it could never be built). Some tiles 2 away from the river are floodplain too. |
| Starting Harmony | Green land is placed until meadow + 2 × woodland equals the biome's starting Harmony (18), so every seed starts at 18.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Camp             | On plain land 2 to 3 tiles from the river, in the middle rows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Randomness       | The map uses its own seeded stream (`seed:map`), so a run's commands never change its map. The run's draft and storm choices use a second stream (`seed:run`) kept in the state.                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Balance simulator (Milestone 2)

| Topic          | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bots           | The design asks for greedy food, greedy energy and random. A fourth, **balanced**, is a steadier baseline (needs first, then Harmony and wellbeing). The three non-random bots share a survival layer: fix this season's power shortfall, keep a food buffer, house growth, compost scraps.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Lookahead      | Ending a season is a pure function, so bots test a fix by ending the season on a copy and reading the report, then keep the build only if it helped (the same as a free undo). By default bots see what a player sees, the forecast (`--sight forecast`); `--sight outcome` lets them see the season exactly as it will resolve, the storm's target included. Over 300 seeds per bot the two differ very little (blackout seasons 7.0 against 6.7 for greedyFood; vision pass rates within 5 points), because a storm disables only one exposed building. |
| Score          | Provisional, until Milestone 7 settles the real formula: 1 per season survived, 2 per citizen, 1 per Harmony, 1 per 10 wellbeing, +20 for finishing. Weights are in the content file (`provisionalScore`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Food buffer    | The bots keep one season of eating in store (two before winter), capped at what storage holds. Until this fix they kept two (three before winter), which outgrew storage late in a run, so they kept building farms they could never fill and the surplus rotted. The food question is answered from the bots that build food only to need; greedyFood spends everything on food on purpose.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Idle season    | A season in which the bot built or changed nothing besides its draft pick.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Collapse cause | The biggest wellbeing drain (hunger, unpowered homes or clutter) over a collapsed run's last 4 seasons. Wellbeing lines now carry a `kind` for this.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Card lift      | Mean score of runs that picked a card in the first 2 years minus runs that did not, among runs that lasted 2 years, averaged over bots. Counting all picks instead would favour every card, because longer runs pick more cards.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Seeds          | Every bot plays the same seeds (`balance-0`, `balance-1`, ...). Results don't depend on the number of threads.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Speed          | States are copied by hand (not `structuredClone`) and write-once parts are shared between copies, which roughly halved the time per run.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Map and placement (Milestone 3)

| Topic             | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Renderer          | PixiJS draws the map; Preact draws the panels. The renderer only reads state; the game store (`src/game/store.ts`) turns clicks into simulation commands.                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## Interface (Milestone 4)

| Topic           | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Layout          | As the mockup: the year strip, Harmony, wellbeing and citizens on top; stores and last season on the left; the map in the middle; the draft, palette and placement or inspector on the right; the forecast, Undo and End season at the bottom.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Year strip      | Past seasons of this year come from the run's history. The current season shows what ending it now would do. Later seasons are forecasts "with what you have now": the same simulation run for that season with today's buildings (`projectSeason`). Shortfalls are striped, not only red.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Tooltips        | Every number on the top bar and every store has a tooltip with its full math, on hover and on keyboard focus: energy by source and by consumer (with heat pumps, free heat, storage and workshop runs), each store's sources this season, the wellbeing lines, the Harmony breakdown, and why the population did or didn't grow. Buildings on the map show their math on hover and open an inspector on click. Tooltips update live while open. |
| Inspector       | Shows a building's status and math for this season, and its controls: the workshop's recipe (salvage or clutter), a digester's slot, and its blackout priority (raise or lower).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Keyboard        | 1–4 pick a card, R rerolls, X buys a 4th card, palette letters pick a building, K spreads compost, arrow keys aim while placing (pan otherwise), N / Shift+N step through legal sites, Enter places, Esc stops, Z or Ctrl+Z undoes, E ends the season, + / − / 0 zoom, ? shows the keys. Remapping comes with Milestone 10.                                                                                                                     |
| Palette letters | The first free letter of each building's name, skipping the global keys, in palette order (starters first). With 20 free letters, a very late-game palette can have a building without a letter; Tab and Enter still reach it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Every legal site is still in the cycle.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Draft cards     | Stained-glass cards with the building's icon on a jewel hex coloured by kind (gold for food and homes, blue for energy and water, rose for storage and civic, green for industry and nature). Their text comes from the building data, so it can't drift from the rules.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Icons           | Building icons are rendered at start-up from the same Pixi art as the map.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| End of run      | A stained-glass summary with the provisional score, a new run, or a last look at the valley. Scoring and the Graft come with Milestone 7.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Not yet         | Nothing: loops, charters and the Almanac came with Milestone 6.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

## Season resolution (Milestone 5)

| Topic                   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Length                  | 5 seconds: the event 0.8 s, day 2.4 s, night 1.2 s, settling 0.6 s. With reduced motion (the system setting) it takes 1.2 s, without drifting, shaking or particle trails.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| What drives it          | The resolution is built from the season report alone (`src/game/timeline.ts`), so every pop, flow and shadow is something the rules did. The simulation has already resolved the season when the animation starts; the animation never changes state.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Skipping                | Space, Esc, E, the Skip button or the banner's Skip end it at once. Any action (a command, picking up a tool, clicking the map) skips the rest first. E only skips while a season plays out, so a double press never ends two seasons. P or the banner's button pauses.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Order                   | The event first (the flood spreads tile by tile along the flood order; storms shake the map; freeze tints it). Then the sun crosses from east (right) to west (left) and each building's yield pops when the sun is over it. Energy flows as light from each source to the consumers nearest it, day and night. At night homes light up and blacked-out buildings go dark. Citizens and wellbeing changes pop at the Founders' Camp as the season settles. |
| Energy flow             | The report has totals per source and per consumer, not who powered whom, so the animation matches each consumer to the nearest sources with energy left. The amounts are right; the pairing is illustrative.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Shadows                 | The shade rule is directionless: any tall neighbour or woodland shades a solar canopy or collector. The animation shows moving shadows from every tall building and woodland tile, falling away from the sun, and keeps each shaded panel in shadow all day with a line to what shades it. The report now names the tiles casting the shade (`report.shaded`).                                                                                             |
| Year strip              | While a season plays out, the strip shows the year as it was, with that season's day slot filling during the day and its night slot at night.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Seasons repaint the map | Spring blossom, a summer warmth, autumn leaves and a warm wash, winter snow and frost on the river banks. Farms the flood fed glitter with silt while the silt bonus lasts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Wildlife                | Birds from the second Harmony tier (20), deer from the third (40), otters from the fourth (70), on free woodland, meadow and river tiles chosen by the tile's own random stream, so they never jump about.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Slow frames             | The season runs on wall-clock time, so a slow frame never stretches it; a frame that jumps over a phase still announces every phase it passed, in order. The ground and buildings are cached as textures (redrawn when they change or the zoom settles), so a frame is cheap even without a GPU.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Not yet                 | Sound (Milestone 9).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

## Combos (Milestone 6)

| Topic             | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data              | Every combo, tuning and charter is in the content file (`combos`, `tunings`, `charters`), validated at load. Behaviour a number can't express is a named module with its parameters in the data: adjacency (`building`, `nextTo`), chains (`links`), formations (`ring`, `line`, `strip`) and evolutions (`nextTo`, `ruinExhausted`, `placed`).                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Discovery         | Step 10 records every combo at work this season (`report.combos`) and the ones found for the first time this run (`report.discoveries`, `state.discoveries`). The Almanac keeps discoveries across runs in the browser; the stained-glass card shows only for combos new to the Almanac.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Adjacency         | The four adjacency rules already lived on the buildings (Milestone 1). A combo only notices them at work: the building is operating and has the right neighbours (Busy Bees only spring to autumn, and not beside a wind spire). They show in the placement preview.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Chains            | A loop closes when it runs: a path of neighbours, one from each link, that all worked this season (made something, ran, or generated). The Gas Loop's digester must feed the night slot. From the next season on, each building in the loop makes +1 of the first of food or compost it makes that season, while every member still stands. A loop is identified by its first building; neighbours that later join it get the bonus too.                                                                                                          |
| Formations        | Found at the start of each season's resolution, so their effect applies to the season they stand in. Village Green: a working Commons Plaza with all 6 neighbours built, of at least 3 types: +2 wellbeing a season. Sun Terrace: 3 canopies in a straight line on hills. Mill Race: weir, river wheel and workshop in a straight line, in that order. ## Open questions for the design

- **Q1. Resolved** (see "Changes to the design" below): food rot fed clutter without limit, and
  scripted runs collapsed by years 4 to 8.
- **Q2. Answered.** DESIGN.md's season order is rewritten to match the simulation. Changes a
  player would notice, compared with the old text: storage set aside for a night it can already see
  comes before workshop and kiln runs (C1); scraps become clutter a season after they are made (C3);
  buildings that need power yield only when powered; heat is paid by free heat, then heat pumps,
  then energy; and step 10 also evolves buildings and checks the vision and era goal.
- **Q3. Answered.** The Mixed Grid counts built sources only (see Energy).
- **Q4. Answered.** The Sun Terrace's "no self-shading" was flavour (C8).
- **Q5. Answered.** The freeze damages nothing, on purpose; the Mixed Grid now also adds energy (see Energy).
- **Q6. Answered.** The Agrivoltaic Field keeps the canopy's output (C11).
- **Q7. Answered.** Repair Culture now improves clutter recycling (C9); the Sun Terrace is +1 day energy (C8).
- **Q8. Answered: food is not too easy.** The design asks whether food is too easy after Year 1.
  It looked so (1.77× made over eaten, 41% rotting), but that was the bots' food buffer outgrowing
  storage. With a buffer they can store, the bots that build food only to need make 1.13× what
  they eat and 8% rots. Nobody they manage goes hungry, because they add farms as people arrive,
  and the random bot, which doesn't, collapses from hunger in 563 of its 707 collapses. No numbers
  changed. See [balance/baseline-report.md](balance/baseline-report.md) and
  [balance/history/03-before-bot-food-buffer.md](balance/history/03-before-bot-food-buffer.md).
- **Q9. Defaults in place.** Era goals use the designer's defaults (see Run structure) until
  playtesting. From the simulator: the Settle and Mend goals are met in almost every competent run;
  Flourish's Harmony 40 only by the Harmony-minded bot; Bloom's year without a shortfall by 89–100%.
- **Q10. Waiting on playtesting.** With bots that see only the forecast, Lantern of the Valley is
  still met by 95–100% of competent runs (around year 8) and Thriving Commons by 13–100% depending
  on how fast a bot grows; Restore the Reach only by the Harmony-minded bot. Bots read every
  forecast and fix each shortfall before ending a season; a player who does the same will find
  Lantern easy too.
- **Q11.** The score formula and the tier bands are open in the design. Are the weights and bands in
  "Run structure" the intended balance between size and restoration?
- **Q12. When does the ending come?** With the first Seeds table, one Graft every run filled the 18
  slots at run 18 for every kind of player. Seeds now scale with the score and a Graft costs 35
  (the Seeds row above), so a run that scores below 375 banks its Seeds instead. `scripts/progression.ts`
  plays simulator run outcomes (refinements, projects and expectations included) into a city with
  these numbers:

  | Player                                                         | Runs to the ending (p10 / median / p90) | Source                   |
  | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
  | Skilled (balanced bot)                                         | 18 / 18 / 19                            | `scripts/progression.ts` |
  | Steady (greedyEnergy bot)                                      | 33 / 35 / 39                            | `scripts/progression.ts` |
  | Learner (random for 3 runs, greedyEnergy for 5, then balanced) | 21 / 21 / 22                            | `scripts/progression.ts` |

  The design aims at 20 to 25 runs. A skilled player still finishes a little early, because the
  best runs' scores are bunched (a Graft cost of 36 would mean 19 runs for them and 22 for the
  learner, but then only 18% of runs pay for a Graft alone). Is 35 right, or should the cost or
  the ending move once people have played it?

- **Q13. The Root City content the design names but doesn't define.** The Spillway, Cider Press,
  Rewilders and Kiln Loop cards, three of the four twists, and four of the five city requests are
  proposals (see "Root City" above). Are these the intended rules?
- **Q14. Two perks read oddly.** The Foundry District's perk is the same at all three tiers in the
  design's table (1 extra workshop run in year 1), so raising it does nothing; should it grow
  (2 runs at Heartwood, or the first 2 years)? And Mended Commons' "start with +4 Harmony" is
  played as +4 Harmony all run; is that the intent?
- **Q15. The late-run content is all proposals.** Refinements, projects, rising expectations and
  harsher seasons answer the playtest finding that the last years had little to do, but the design
  defines none of them. Are these the intended shapes, and do their numbers feel right in play?
- **Q16. Regions and the new twists are proposals.** The design names regions only as a map seed;
  the seven valleys and six new twists are mine, and the hard twists' new numbers too (made harsher
  after playtesting, see Hard twists). Do they feel right in play?
